Support can be found in Ganymede by clicking on the <div className="button darkblue_button"><QuestionCircleOutlined /></div> icon in the top right corner of the app.

Clicking on the icon opens a dropdown menu with the following options:
- **Documentation** - Open this documentation site
- [**Product Tours**](#product-tours) - Replay a guided walkthrough
- [**Contact Support**](#ganymede-support) - Open the Apprentice support portal

<img alt="Support Modal" src="https://storage.googleapis.com/ganymede-bio-website/public/apiServer/SupportModal_20241206.png"/>
<br /><br />

### Ganymede Support

Select **Contact Support** to open the Apprentice support portal in a new browser tab. Use the portal to get help with any issues you may be experiencing or to report a bug.
